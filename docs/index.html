Pocket Book
En français Source code

Passwords and contacts, in large print.

A simple address book for your passwords and the people you call. It works without internet, without an account and without a master password to forget.

Download Pocket Book Other systems

Free and open source. Windows, macOS and Linux. In English, French, Spanish, German and Italian.

F

Features

  • Password cards with the website, username, email and password. The password is locked so it cannot be erased by mistake.
  • Contact cards with mobile, landline, email, postal address and birthday.
  • A password generator that makes passwords from easy words in your language, or very strong ones.
  • Your old Word and text files become tidy cards. Drag them onto the window: Pocket Book puts each piece of information in the right field and never changes the original files.
  • Saved automatically, with a backup every day and a readable .txt copy of every card.
  • Easy on the eyes: four text sizes, four colour themes including high contrast, an A to Z letter bar and search.
I

Install

Windows

  1. Double-click PocketBook-Setup-Windows.exe.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click Install. An icon appears on the Desktop and in the Start menu.

Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .zip file.
  2. Drag Pocket Book into Applications.
  3. The first time, if the Mac refuses to open it: go to System Settings, Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.

Linux

  1. Extract PocketBook-Linux.tar.gz.
  2. Double-click pocket-book, then click Install. It is added to your applications menu.

These warnings appear because the app is free and not signed with a paid certificate.

P

Privacy

Pocket Book never connects to the internet by itself. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking: your cards stay in the Documents/Pocket Book folder of your computer. To move to another computer, copy that folder.

Pocket Book is not an encrypted vault. Anyone who can open your user account can read your cards. It replaces “passwords in Word documents”, not a vault like Bitwarden or KeePass. Protect your computer with a session password.
Q

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. Pocket Book is free and open source, under the MIT licence.
Who is it for?
People who are not at ease with computers, and the family members who set up their computer. It was first made for an 80-year-old grandmother and her 1,500 Word documents.
Can it read my old .doc files?
It reads .docx and .txt. Open old .doc files in Word and save them as .docx first.
How do I uninstall it?
Windows: Settings, Apps, Pocket Book, Uninstall. Mac: drag the app to the Bin. Linux: Settings inside the app. Your cards are kept unless you choose to delete them.
